Mono that keeps the whole sound.

A small Mac app that batch converts stereo WAV and AIFF to mono for hardware samplers like the Elektron Digitakt, Roland SP-404 and Erica Synths Sample Drum.

$0.99 once · macOS 12 or later

From the Mac App Store.

Open source

Mono, done properly.

Correct mono.

Left and right are summed into one channel, a proper downmix. Nothing is dropped.

A whole folder at once.

Drop in a folder and get it back in mono, its structure kept. Files already there are renamed, never replaced.

The format your sampler wants.

16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float. 44.1, 48 or 96 kHz, or the rate it had. WAV or AIFF.

Drop, choose, done.

  1. Drop files or a folder

    Any number of WAV and AIFF files, folders and all.

  2. Choose the output

    Bit depth, sample rate and format, set once for the whole batch.

  3. Converted

    Every file in mono, in the folders it came from. Nothing overwritten.
