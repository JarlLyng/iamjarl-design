About this page

Three candidates for Walkful's display face, for the owner to choose between (#65). Everything else is the same in all three: the sand ground, charcoal text, one magenta line, the line under the headline from Walkful's current site, the store button and the Today screen. The phone frame is a placeholder for the owner's drawn frame, and the magenta line stands in for the drawings.

Files: Fraunces with its soft axis (62 KB), Young Serif in its one weight (27 KB) and Newsreader (58 KB), all OFL-1.1 from Fontsource 5.3.0, in fonts/. The two not chosen leave before the release.
